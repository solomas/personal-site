## What changed

- `src/styles/tokens.css`: the glass tokens and the sheet shadows are gone. New band tokens: `--band`, `--band-grain`, `--on-band`, `--on-band-muted`, `--band-hairline`, `--band-marker`, `--notch` and `--paper-texture`, with night values.
- `src/styles/global.css`: `.glass` and `.glass-panel` now draw a band, with muted text, hairlines and the highlighter stroke switched to values that read on it. `.glass-button` is a square block in the band colour, on a band it takes the ground colour. `.sheet` is flat with a hairline. A new `.notched` class draws the notch and the arrow in CSS. The backdrop filter fallbacks are removed.
- `src/pages/index.astro`: a two column home page, the hero on the left with the kicker "Planetary health researcher, Lisbon" from the page title, the bands on the right. One column below 900px.
- `src/components/HomeSheets.astro`: the three streams as notched bands with a small number. The first visit reveal now slides the bands in from the right.
- `src/components/Layout.astro`: the nav has no glass bar any more and its gutter matches the page content.
- `src/components/EntryList.astro`: every entry is a notched band, Other work is a band of its own.
- `src/components/ThesisPanel.astro`: hover turns the action button ink instead of lifting the panel.
- `src/components/ReadingLayout.astro`, `StreamHeader.astro`, `EntryLayout.astro`, `src/pages/about.astro`: square strip and updated comments.
- `CLAUDE.md`: the design token section describes the bands instead of the glass.
