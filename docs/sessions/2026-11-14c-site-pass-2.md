## What changed

- Bands: a flat full colour. The ink grain is gone from the bands, the shapes keep theirs. `--band-grain` is removed from `tokens.css` and `global.css`.
- The notch is now a real bite: the band's own background leaves a transparent half circle, so the paper and the shapes show through it. The disc in the ground colour and its ink fill on hover are gone. Hover and focus move the arrow a little. `--paper-texture` is removed from the tokens.
- `src/components/StreamLayout.astro` (new): the frame of the three stream index pages. Title and intro sit on the left and stay in view on wide screens, the bands sit on the right. One column below 960px.
- `StreamHeader.astro` lost its own page padding and sits inside StreamLayout, with a smaller title on wide screens.
- `EntryList.astro`: date, title and summary stack inside each band. `--entry-date-width` is removed from the tokens.
- `ThesisPanel.astro`: sized for the right column.
- `src/pages/work/governance-scan.astro` (new): the Governance Scan offer, in the reading frame. What it is, who it is for, the five dimensions in plain words, how the six weeks run, the research behind it and how to get in touch. No price. It states that client work never becomes a thesis case.
- `src/components/OfferBand.astro` (new): a notched band at the top of the work page that links to the scan page.
- `sitemap.xml.ts` lists the scan page. The highlighter exclusions in `global.css` name `.offer-band` and no longer name `.deck`.
- `src/components/DeckPanel.astro` removed, it was unused.
- `CLAUDE.md`: the design token section says the bands are flat, without grain.
